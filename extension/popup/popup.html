Reading style

Weight: important words get heavier bold. Tree: the deeper a word sits in the sentence's grammar tree, the lighter it is. Indent: sentences of more than 8 words get one line per clause, with each clause that modifies another indented one step.

Page text style

Original keeps the site's own font. Rounded and Editorial change only the text REden formats.
