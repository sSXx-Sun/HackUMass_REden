Find the core. Follow the meaning.

REden helps you focus on the words that carry a sentence’s meaning. By bringing its essential structure into view or gently softening less important words, it is designed to support faster reading and clearer understanding—while keeping the full text in place.

In development

Emphasis that learns what’s familiar to you.

We’re developing a personal word-frequency system to help tailor bolding to each reader. The goal is to adjust emphasis around the words you encounter often and those that may need more attention.

Future direction

More languages. More ways to understand.

Our long-term goal is to bring REden to more languages, with reading support that respects their different structures. We also want to explore how personalized emphasis can help people studying another language.

Languages build sentences differently, so subject–verb–object will not apply the same way everywhere.
